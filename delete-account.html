Delete your account

Last updated 5 November 2026

You can delete your Hortlog account and personal data yourself, at any time. You do not need to contact us.

In the app

  1. Open Hortlog and log in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Find Help and your data and tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

Download your records first if you want a copy. Settings has Excel and PDF exports.

Cannot get into the app?

Email pentreathliam@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up, with the subject Delete my Hortlog account. We will delete it and reply within 20 working days.

What is deleted

  • Your login, name, notification details and any feedback you sent.
  • If you are the only person in an orchard, that orchard is deleted too, with all of its records, photos and signing files.

What is kept

  • If other people use an orchard you own, we ask you to delete the orchard or remove everyone else first, so nobody loses their records.
  • Records you added to an orchard owned by someone else stay with that orchard, because they are part of its audit history. They no longer link to you.
  • Documents signed through a link belong to the orchard that sent them. To have yours removed, email us.
  • Copies may stay in our providers’ backups for a limited time before they are overwritten.

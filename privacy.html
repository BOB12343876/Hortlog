Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Hortlog is an orchard record-keeping app made by [YOUR NAME OR BUSINESS NAME] in New Zealand. This policy explains what information Hortlog collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it. We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. If you have a question, email [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS].

What we collect

WhatWhy
Your account: email address, your name, and your role in the orchard. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google email address and name.To log you in and show your team who you are.
Your orchard records: jobs, notes, sprays and fertiliser, monitoring, blocks, map areas, photos you add, products and settings.This is the core of the app. It lets you keep, sync and export your records.
Your team: names and roles of people in your orchard, and the email addresses you invite.To give each person the right access and send invitations.
Documents sent for signing: the document, the name and signature of the person who signs, their optional notes, and the time they signed.To record that a document was signed. The person signing does not need an account.
Notification details: a code from your phone’s browser that lets us send you notifications, and your time zone.Only if you turn notifications on. To send the alerts and reminders you choose.
Feedback you send us: your message, the app version and your phone’s browser details.To fix problems and improve Hortlog.
Your location: only when you tap “My location” on the map and allow it in your browser. The map area you choose for a block may be saved with that block.To centre the map on your orchard.

Hortlog has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell your information.

Voice and photos

When you log by voice, your phone or browser’s own speech recognition turns your speech into text. On Chrome and Android, that service is run by Google and may process your audio under Google’s own privacy policy. Hortlog only receives the text. We do not record or store audio. Photos you add are stored with your orchard records.

Who can see your records

Only people in your orchard can see your orchard’s records, and each person only sees what their role allows. For example, a contractor sees only the blocks they were given and cannot see spray records. The Hortlog team does not look at your records except when needed to fix a problem you ask us about, or where the law requires it. Orchard owners and managers can see the email addresses of people in their orchard.

Services we use

We use these companies to run Hortlog. They only handle information to provide their service to us.

  • Supabase: database, login and file storage.
  • Vercel: hosting the app and this website.
  • Google: “Continue with Google” sign-in (if you use it), fonts, and speech recognition in Chrome.
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs) and jsDelivr: delivering code libraries the app needs.
  • Land Information New Zealand (LINZ): aerial map pictures, which are requested for the area you are viewing.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): finding an address when you search for one on the map.
  • Push notification services run by Google, Apple or Mozilla, which deliver notifications to your phone.

Some of these providers store information outside New Zealand. Our database is hosted by Supabase in [SUPABASE REGION, e.g. Sydney, Australia]. By using Hortlog you understand your information may be handled overseas by these providers.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account (see below) your personal information is removed. Copies may stay in our providers’ backups for a limited time before they are overwritten.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself at any time: open Hortlog, go to Settings, find Help and your data and tap Delete my account. You can also email [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS] and we will do it for you.

  • Your login, name, notification details and feedback are deleted.
  • If you are the only person in an orchard, that orchard and all of its records, photos and signing files are deleted too.
  • If other people use an orchard you own, we ask you to delete the orchard or remove everyone else first.
  • Records you added to an orchard owned by someone else stay with that orchard, because they are part of its audit history. They no longer link to you.
  • Documents signed through a link belong to the orchard that sent them. To have yours removed, email [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS].

Download your records first if you want a copy. Settings has Excel and PDF exports.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Email [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS] and we will reply within 20 working days. If you are not happy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections. Each orchard’s records are separated so that one orchard cannot read another’s. No system is perfectly secure. If you find a problem, please tell us at [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS].

Children

Hortlog is for people who work in orchards and is not meant for children under 16.
